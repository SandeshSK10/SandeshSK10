## 🔬 Featured: Trustworthy RAG (Master's dissertation, 2026)

A research prototype for human review of medication information, built around one question:
**how does an AI system prove where its answer came from, and admit when it can't answer?**

| Design principle | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Separate logic from language** | Deterministic guideline assessment is kept apart from LLM-written explanations |
| **Attributed evidence** | Every claim is linked to separately attributed guideline sources |
| **Citation checks** | Explanations are checked against the evidence they cite |
| **Abstention** | The system declines to answer when evidence is missing |
| **Human in the loop** | Missing information is flagged and a person confirms before anything proceeds |

I am evaluating retrieval quality, explanation support and workflow failures.
> ⚠️ This is an academic prototype. It is **not clinically validated** and does not prescribe or change medicines.
